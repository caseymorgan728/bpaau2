KYC

KYC Verification Checklist for Fast PayID payouts

Identity verification is the single biggest determinant of how fast you get paid, and the only part of the process you fully control. Complete it immediately after registering rather than at cashout — the requirement does not go away, it only becomes urgent at the worst time. Most Gaming Curacao licensed operators require: (1) Government-issued ID — passport, driver's licence, or national ID card (clear photo of both sides), (2) Proof of address — utility bill, bank statement, or rental agreement under 3 months old with your name and address, (3) Proof of payment method — sometimes a screenshot of your PayID or bank statement showing the linked account.

Additional checks may include: (4) Selfie holding your ID (for higher withdrawal amounts), (5) Source of funds documentation (for very large withdrawals), (6) Email/phone verification — click the link in the verification email and confirm your mobile number. Tips: upload clear, well-lit photos; ensure all details are legible; use the same name on all documents and your casino/PayID account. Most operators process KYC within 24–48 hours. Timelines

PayID Withdrawal Timelines: What to Expect in 2026

Timelines cluster into predictable bands once you know what drives them. A first withdrawal runs 24–72 hours because it absorbs identity checks alongside the payment. Later withdrawals on a verified account typically land in 1–24 hours, and a well-run operator will do it inside the hour. Weekends add time at brands that batch approvals around staffed shifts rather than processing continuously — the NPP itself runs every day of the year, so any weekend delay is the operator's roster, not the banking system. Larger amounts commonly trip a manual review threshold and can take 3–5 business days, which is worth knowing before you request one big payout instead of two smaller ones.

Factors that affect speed: (1) KYC completion — verified accounts are faster, (2) Withdrawal amount — smaller amounts process quicker, (3) Operator processing time — some process withdrawals instantly, others have a 24-hour pending period, (4) Bank processing — PayID transfers are usually instant once the casino releases funds, (5) Time of day — requests during business hours process faster. Always check the operator's current withdrawal policy, as times can change. No deposit winnings are capped at max cashout ($100–$250), so these withdrawals are typically processed quickly.

Does a bank gambling block stop PayID deposits?

Usually not. ANZ may simply show an optional gambling transfer warning rather than refuse the payment.
