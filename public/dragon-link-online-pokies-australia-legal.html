Quick answer

The Short Version

There is no authorised real-money online Dragon Link for Australian players. The genuine game is an Aristocrat cabinet in licensed land venues; the mobile apps are social casinos with non-redeemable coins; and Australia-facing “real Dragon Link online” sites are unlicensed clones. For comparable Asian-themed jackpot pokies online, use verified casinos offering games such as 5 Dragons and Wolf Treasure.

The real game

What Dragon Link Is

Dragon Link is Aristocrat’s linked progressive series built on the same Hold & Spin engine as Lightning Link, with Asian-themed titles including Panda Magic, Golden Century, Peace & Long Life, Spring Festival, Genghis Khan, Autumn Moon, Happy & Prosperous, Silk Road and Peacock Princess. Landing six or more golden orb symbols locks them for a respin sequence; collecting more orbs builds prize tiers, and filling the screen awards the linked grand jackpot shared across the bank of machines.

Like Lightning Link it is a land-based, venue-linked product, which determines where it can legally be played for cash.

Legal play

Where Real Dragon Link Is Legal in Australia

Dragon Link cabinets operate in state- and territory-licensed clubs, hotels/pubs and casinos. Machine return settings, jackpot rules and player protections are set and audited under state gambling legislation. There is no Aristocrat-licensed Australian online equivalent.

The apps

Social Casino Apps Are Not Real Money

Aristocrat’s social titles (Heart of Vegas and Cashman Casino, via Product Madness) include Dragon Link-style games. They award virtual coins only, contain no cash-out mechanism and are funded by coin purchases. They are an entertainment product for adults, not a route to PayID winnings — any site suggesting otherwise is misleading you.

Overseas & clones

Regulated Overseas Markets vs Australian Clone Sites

Aristocrat supplies real-money digital content to selected regulated markets abroad, but those casinos legally block Australian addresses and require local identity and banking. In parallel, a large number of unauthorised clone sites target Australians using Dragon Link art and the Hold & Spin name. Red flags mirror those for Lightning Link clones: odd domains, no certified RNG, no verifiable licence, and withdrawals blocked behind escalating fees. Learn to authenticate operators on the licence-check guide.

The law

Interactive Gambling Act in Plain Terms

The Interactive Gambling Act 2001 prohibits supplying online casino/pokie services to Australians without the relevant authority; ACMA coordinates blocking of non-compliant sites. Enforcement is aimed at operators rather than individual adults, but an unlicensed clone offers no Australian legal recourse if it withholds your money. FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Australians play Dragon Link online for real money?
No authorised real-money online version of Dragon Link is offered to players in Australia. Genuine Dragon Link is a land-based Aristocrat machine in licensed clubs, pubs and casinos. Online real-money versions exist only in certain regulated overseas markets and are geo-blocked for Australia.
Is Dragon Link the same as Lightning Link?
They are related Aristocrat families sharing the Hold & Spin lock-and-respin mechanic and linked progressive jackpots. Dragon Link uses Asian-themed titles (such as Panda Magic, Golden Century, Autumn Moon and Happy & Prosperous) and a golden-orb bonus symbol, while Lightning Link covers a wider set of themes with a pearl symbol. Both are venue-based products in Australia.
Do the Heart of Vegas or Cashman apps pay real money?
No. They are free-to-play social casinos using virtual coins with no cash-redemption value, supported by in-app purchases. They let you enjoy Dragon Link-style features for fun but cannot pay Australian dollars to PayID or a bank account.
Why do some websites claim to offer real Dragon Link online?
They are unauthorised clones. Aristocrat does not license a real-money online Dragon Link for Australia, so any Australia-facing site using its name and artwork for cash bets lacks certification and a valid licence, and commonly withholds withdrawals or charges fake fees.
Where is real Dragon Link legal to play in Australia?
On Aristocrat cabinets in state-licensed clubs, hotels and casinos. These venues operate under state gambling law with regulated machine settings and consumer protections.
What online pokies are similar to Dragon Link?
Asian-themed and hold-and-respin jackpot pokies are the closest online equivalents — for example 5 Dragons and the Money Respin game Wolf Treasure, plus the broader hold-and-win and progressive-jackpot ranges at verified Australian-facing casinos.
Is playing at an offshore casino illegal for me personally?
The Interactive Gambling Act 2001 primarily targets operators providing prohibited services to Australians; ACMA blocks non-compliant sites. The main risk to players is dealing with unlicensed operators that may not pay or protect data, which is why using licence-verified sites matters more than the theoretical legal status of the player.
How do I report a fake Dragon Link site?
Stop paying, screenshot everything, alert your bank or PayID provider, and report the site to ACMA and Scamwatch. Never send further money to unlock a withdrawal.
