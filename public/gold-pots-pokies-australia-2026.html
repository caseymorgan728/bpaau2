Features

Bonus Features, Free Spins & Special Symbols

Gold Pots' appeal is in its money-collection and pot mechanic rather than a long list of unrelated bonus rounds:
  • Gold Pot Money Symbols: Pot symbols that land carry a cash value and stick on the reels for a limited number of respins, filling the pot meter.
  • Progressive-Style Pots: Collected pots pay out at the end of the collection phase - this is where the $2,800+ ticker hits come from.
  • Respins: Landing new pot symbols resets or extends the respin counter, keeping the feature alive.
  • Wild Substitution: Standard wilds substitute for pay symbols in the base game.
The pots are not a single pooled network jackpot; they are bet-multiplier cash values accumulated within a feature round. Higher bets mean higher pot values, which is why the ticker shows four-figure wins.
Strategy

How do the gold pots bonus work in Gold Pots?

Gold Pot money symbols land on the reels and stick during a respin feature, each carrying a cash value tied to your bet. They accumulate toward the pot meter; when the collection phase ends, all collected pot values pay out. This respin-and-collect mechanic is where the recent $2,800-$2,886 AUD wins on the shared ticker originate.

With no published RTP for the exact title, treat Gold Pots as a medium-volatility pot-collection pokie. The key strategic point is that the pot bonus is a bet-multiplier event: the pot values scale with your stake, so playing at a cent-level bet will produce cent-level pots even when the feature lands.
