Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

BPAAU (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and disclose information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to browse our site. We may collect non-personal information such as your browser type, device type, pages visited and referring URLs through standard server logs and privacy-friendly analytics tools. We do not use cookies to track you across third-party websites.

The categories of information we collect are limited and, wherever possible, anonymised. They include: analytics data such as which pages you view, how long you spend on them and the device and browser you use; cookie data described below; referral click data that records which outbound affiliate link you clicked (but not who you are); and, if you choose to email us, the contact details and message contents you voluntarily provide. We do not collect names, addresses, date of birth, financial account numbers or any other sensitive personal information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use aggregated, non-personal information to understand how visitors use our site, improve our content and fix technical issues. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Specifically, we use the information we collect to: maintain and improve site performance and content; understand which guides and comparisons our Australian readers find useful; attribute outbound clicks to the relevant affiliate network so we receive the referral commission that keeps the site free; and respond to inquiries you send us by email. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymised before it is reviewed, and we do not use it to build individual advertising profiles or to retarget you off-site.

Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party online casinos. When you click these links and visit a casino, that casino’s own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use minimal cookies only for essential site functionality. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

First-party cookies may be set by our hosting provider for basic security and load-balancing purposes. Privacy-friendly analytics tools (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and does not fingerprint individual visitors) record aggregate page-view data. Affiliate tracking cookies may be set when you click an outbound link to a listed operator; these are placed by the operator or its affiliate network, not by us, and they record only that a click originated from this site. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings (under Settings, Privacy and Security in Chrome, or Preferences, Privacy in Safari). Disabling cookies will not prevent you from reading our content, though it may affect how affiliate attribution is recorded.

Third-Party Services

Our site is hosted and delivered through Cloudflare, which provides content delivery network (CDN) and DDoS protection and may log basic request data such as IP address and user-agent for security purposes. Because this is a zero-JavaScript site, we do not load Google Analytics or any third-party JavaScript tracker. Outbound links to online casinos are managed through affiliate networks; once you leave this site, the operator's own privacy policy and cookie practices apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Data Security

All traffic to bpaau.org is served over HTTPS, and our hosting is protected by Cloudflare's security layer, which includes automatic encryption and mitigation of common attacks. We do not store passwords, credit card numbers, bank account details or any other sensitive financial information on our servers, and we never ask you to enter gambling-related personal data on this website. Referral clicks are anonymised; we receive only aggregate click and conversion reports from affiliate networks, not individual player records.

Your Privacy Rights (Australia)

BPAAU handles personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) set out in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). If you are an Australian resident, you have the right to request access to any personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it. Because the site is largely anonymous, most visitors will not have identifiable personal information on our systems. If you have emailed us, you may request a copy of your message or ask us to delete it by contacting the address below. We will respond within a reasonable period. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Children's Privacy

BPAAU is intended strictly for users aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy was last updated in January 2026. We may update it from time to time to reflect changes in our services, hosting providers or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will revise the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@bpaau.org.
