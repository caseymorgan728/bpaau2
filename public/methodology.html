How We Test & Rate Online Pokies Casinos

Every casino we review goes through the same 6-step testing process. No shortcuts, no sponsored scores, no paid rankings. Here is exactly what we do.

Our 6-Step Testing Process

1

Licence & Background Check

We verify the operator’s gaming licence, check the regulator register (Gaming Curacao), review ownership history, and look for any past regulatory actions or player complaints. A casino without a valid, verifiable licence never makes our list.

2

Bonus & Promotion Audit

We claim every available bonus, read the full terms and conditions, and calculate the real wagering requirement. We check game weighting, max bet limits, expiry dates and withdrawal caps. Bonuses that look generous but are effectively unplayable get flagged.

3

Game Selection & Pokie Testing

We browse the full pokie library, count total titles, check software providers, and play a range of pokies from classic 3-reel to modern Megaways. We test loading speed, RNG fairness indicators and mobile compatibility.

4

Deposit & Withdrawal Test

We deposit via multiple methods (PayID, POLi, Neosurf, crypto) and request real-money withdrawals. We track every step: KYC verification, processing time, and time to funds arrival. Slow or blocked withdrawals are a major penalty.

5

Customer Support Test

We contact live chat, email and phone support at different times (including late night and weekends) with real questions. We measure response time, accuracy, and whether the agent resolves the issue or just gives a scripted answer.

6

Responsible Gambling & Fairness Check

We verify that the casino offers deposit limits, self-exclusion links, reality checks and access to support resources. We also check that games are audited by independent testing labs (eCOGRA, iTech Labs, GLI) for RNG fairness.

Our Scoring Principles

Independence

Our scores are never for sale. Commercial partnerships do not influence ratings. We accept free test accounts but always use our own real money for withdrawal verification.

Transparency

We publish exactly how we score, what we test and why a casino received a particular rating. If we deduct points, we explain why.

Recency

Casinos change. We re-test every recommended operator quarterly and update scores when terms, bonuses or payout speeds change materially.

Player-First

We evaluate from the perspective of an Australian pokie player. What matters to you — fast PayID withdrawals, fair wagering, good pokies — matters most to our scoring.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you score online casinos?

We score every operator across six weighted categories: Licensing & Trust (25%), Bonuses & Promotions (20%), Game Selection (20%), Payments & Withdrawals (15%), Customer Support (10%) and Responsible Gambling & Fairness (10%). Each category is scored out of 100 by the relevant team member, then weighted to produce an overall score out of 100. A 90+ score means a recommended top-tier casino, while anything below 60 is marked not recommended. You can read the full weightings and score bands in the tables above.

Do you deposit real money when testing?

Yes. Every review includes real-money deposits across multiple methods, and we never rely on the operator’s own marketing figures. Our team registers a fresh account, verifies it with real ID, claims the welcome and reload bonuses, plays a spread of pokies across the library, and then requests a real-money withdrawal back into an Australian bank account. We log KYC requests, processing times and any fees ourselves, so the payout speed and support quality we report is what we actually experienced, not what the casino claims on its banking page.

How do you test withdrawal speeds?

Emma logs a withdrawal request the moment wagering is cleared and records three timestamps: submission, operator approval, and funds arrival in our own bank account. We test each supported method — PayID, POLi, Neosurf and crypto — across at least three withdrawal cycles per operator, including a first withdrawal on a freshly verified account, which is almost always slower than later ones. We note any KYC document demands, weekend cut-off times and fees, and we only call a casino “fast” when verified PayID withdrawals land within the 24–72 hour window advertised. See our PayID cashouts guide for the live benchmarks.

What gets an operator delisted?

We remove an operator from our recommended list immediately if we find it operating without a current, verifiable Gaming Curacao licence; if it repeatedly blocks or delays legitimate withdrawals beyond 72 hours after KYC; if it changes bonus terms after a player has claimed; or if we receive and can corroborate serious player complaints about withheld funds. A casino can also be delisted for failing to offer deposit limits, self-exclusion or reality checks. Once delisted, the review is updated to say so, and we do not restore the listing until the issue is independently resolved and re-tested.

How often are reviews updated?

We re-test every recommended operator at least once per quarter, and we refresh scores, bonus assessments and withdrawal speed reports within seven days of any material change — a new licence, an overhaul of the bonus schedule, payout delays or a regulatory action. Between full re-tests, Sarah checks bonus amounts, wagering multipliers and expiry windows weekly against the operators’ current 2026 terms, so the numbers you see reflect the offer on the day you read it rather than a stale snapshot.
