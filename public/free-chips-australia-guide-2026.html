The Basics

What Are Free Chips Australia & How Do They Work?

A no-deposit chip is bonus credit an operator places in your account before you have funded it, usually $10–$50, sometimes issued instead as a set of spins (20–100, most often 77). The mechanical detail that matters is where it lands: a separate bonus wallet, not your cash balance. Money sitting in that wallet cannot be withdrawn under any circumstances — it can only be played. Wagering requirements are simply the condition governing when value is allowed to move from the bonus wallet across into the cash wallet, and until that transfer happens there is genuinely nothing to cash out. Everything else in the terms — eligible games, contribution weighting, the 24–72 hour expiry — is a restriction on how you may attempt that transfer.

The two formats differ in what the wagering multiple is calculated against, and that is the most consequential line in any bonus term. A chip balance is normally wagered on the bonus amount itself: accept $20 at 30x and you owe $600 of turnover whether you run hot or cold. Spins are normally wagered on what they win: turn 77 spins into $12 and the same 30x asks for $360. Neither is inherently better, but they fail differently. A chip commits you to a fixed workload the instant you accept it. Spins scale the workload to your luck, which means a bad run costs you nothing but time. Check whether the terms say "bonus amount" or "winnings" before you decide which is worth an evening. See our bonus types guide for a full comparison.

Terms

Wagering Requirements, Max Cashout & Key Terms Explained

Every no-deposit offer carries the same four terms, and the point most players miss is that they interact. Wagering (20x–40x) sets the turnover. Max cashout ($100–$250) caps what may leave the account no matter what you win. Expiry (24–72 hours) limits when. Game weighting decides what actually counts. Work a real example: a $20 chip at 30x is $600 of turnover. On a pokie contributing 100% at 50c a spin, that is 1,200 spins — long, but a plausible evening. Move to a title contributing 10% and the same $600 obligation now demands $6,000 of betting. That is not a session, it is arithmetic designed never to complete. The multiple on its own never tells you whether a bonus is clearable — the multiple measured against the weighting does.

Game contribution rates determine how much each bet counts toward wagering — slots usually count 100%, while table games and live dealer often count 10% or 0%. Max bet limits during bonus play (often $5–$10) prevent abuse. Eligible games are the only titles you can play with bonus funds. Always read the full bonus policy and keep screenshots of the offer page. If any term looks unclear, contact support before accepting.
