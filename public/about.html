About BPAAU — Independent Pokies Reviews for Australians

We are a team of pokie players, data analysts and iGaming journalists who got tired of affiliate websites ranking casinos based on who pays the highest commission. BPAAU exists to give Australian players honest, tested and useful information about offshore online casinos.

Our Mission

Australian players deserve better than advertorials dressed up as reviews. BPAAU independently tests every casino we feature — we register, deposit, play pokies, claim bonuses, request withdrawals and document every step. Our scores are based on real experience, not marketing sheets.

Bonus & Wagering Analyst

Sarah is our bonus and promotions expert. Frequently Asked Questions

Who runs BPAAU?

BPAAU is run by a four-person editorial team with over 25 years of combined experience in online gaming, data analytics and journalism. Daniel Hart leads reviews and sets the testing standard, Sarah Chen models bonus economics, Marcus Webb evaluates game mechanics and Emma Rodriguez tests payments and withdrawals. We are based in Australia and test every operator from the perspective of a real Australian pokie player using a local PayID account. We are independent of the casinos we review, and no operator has ever told us what to write.

How do you make money, and are your reviews independent?

BPAAU is funded by referral commissions: when you click one of our outbound links and sign up at a listed operator, we may earn a commission. This never influences our ratings, rankings or editorial conclusions. Our team registers, deposits, plays and withdraws with our own real money, and we test every operator on the same six-step process regardless of the commercial arrangement. If a casino pays slowly, offers unfair terms or loses its licence, we say so openly — even when that means removing it from our recommended list. Full details are on our Methodology and in our Terms of Service.

How often do you update your reviews?

We re-test every recommended operator at least once per quarter, and we refresh our scores, bonus assessments and withdrawal speed reports within seven days of any material change. That includes new bonus offers, wagering changes, payout delays, licence updates or any complaint we can independently verify. Our bonus amounts, expiry windows and PayID processing times are checked weekly against the operators’ current 2026 terms, so the figures you read here reflect what is actually available when you visit the site, not what was true six months ago.

Are BPAAU recommendations safe for Australian players?

We only list offshore operators that hold a valid, verifiable Gaming Curacao licence and that we have personally tested end-to-end. Every page displays an 18+ notice and links to free responsible-gambling support, and we publish our self-exclusion and deposit-limit guidance alongside every recommendation. No-deposit bonuses and free chips are for entertainment only — they are not a reliable source of income, and all pokie outcomes are decided by random number generators with a built-in house edge. Always play within your means and set limits before you start.
