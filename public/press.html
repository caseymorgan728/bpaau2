Editorial Independence Statement

BPAAU’s editorial independence is the foundation of everything we publish. No operator has ever influenced a ranking, a review score or the decision to cover or decline coverage of a brand. We receive the same affiliate commission structure from every operator we list, so there is no financial incentive to favour one site over another. Every review is based on real-money testing by our own editors: we register, deposit, play pokies, clear bonus wagering, request withdrawals and verify licences ourselves, and we publish the results — including the negative findings. Operators cannot pay to be listed, cannot pay to remove a negative review, and cannot request changes to our editorial conclusions. When an operator is reviewed poorly or dropped from our coverage, it is because our testing found a genuine problem — a slow or refused withdrawal, a misleading bonus term, or an unverifiable licence. If you are a journalist and want to verify this process, read our Methodology page or contact editor@bpaau.org.
