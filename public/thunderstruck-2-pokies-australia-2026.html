The Game

What Is Thunderstruck II?

Thunderstruck II is a five-reel, 243-ways-to-win video pokie from Microgaming (now part of Games Global), first released in 2010 and still one of the most recognised pokies in Australian pubs, clubs and online. The theme is Norse mythology - Thor, Odin, Loki and Valkyrie - with the Great Hall of Spins as the central bonus. Unlike the JILI/JOKER titles on these lobbies, Microgaming publishes a verified RTP of 96.65%, which is a meaningful advantage for players choosing on return rather than on big-win frequency. Volatility is medium-high, and the max win reaches roughly 5,000x the bet - at the operator's max bet that is the ~$2.4 million headline figure cited in reviews.

The reason Thunderstruck II features in the operators' curated best-pokies lists is exactly this published credibility: it is a globally recognised, audited title rather than a white-label mystery. It appears in the curated lists at 1XAUD, MR BLUEY and TOY STORY9, where it sits behind Wicked Fortune as the recognisable global-brand title. The 243-ways format means there are no fixed paylines to line up - any matching symbols on adjacent reels from the left win, which suits Australian players used to Aristocrat-style multiway pokies. Features

Bonus Features, Free Spins & Special Symbols

Thunderstruck II's four-mode Great Hall of Spins is the reason it has survived 15+ years:
  • Wildstorm: Random base-game feature that turns up to five reels fully wild, creating massive line wins - this is the iconic Thunderstruck moment.
  • Valkyrie (5 spins): First unlocked mode - 10 free spins with a 5x multiplier on all wins.
  • Loki (5th+ entry): 15 free spins with the Wild Magic symbol turning random reels wild.
  • Odin (10th+ entry): 20 free spins with the Raven feature that randomly transforms symbols into 2x or 3x multipliers.
  • Thor (15th+ entry): 25 free spins with rolling reels (Tumbling Reels) that stack multipliers up to 5x per consecutive win.
The progressive unlock means early sessions are smaller, but the long-term ceiling is the Thor mode with rolling multipliers. This is a game that rewards repeated play rather than a single big spin.
