Our Methodology: We Test Cashouts Weekly and Log Timestamps

To rank payout speed honestly you have to measure it, not ask the operator. Every week our reviewers log into a fully KYC-verified account at each of the six operators, request a PayID withdrawal on a weekday morning, and record three timestamps: the moment the request is submitted, the moment the operator moves it from "pending" to "approved", and the moment the AUD clears in our bank feed. We repeat this across multiple weeks so the numbers reflect real variation, not one lucky run.

We report median time (the typical experience), 90th-percentile time (how bad the wait gets one time in ten), and the rejected-payout rate (how often a withdrawal bounced for KYC, name mismatch or an un-wagered bonus). Median tells you the average Saturday experience; the 90th percentile and rejection rate tell you whether a fast headline hides a fragile process. Full detail is on our methodology page. Bank-side PayID is near-instant, so nearly all of the measured delay is the operator's own review queue.

We deliberately keep the test conditions identical each week so the numbers are comparable: same verified account, same PayID destination, a same-size withdrawal in the standard band, and a weekday-morning request window. We never test with an active bonus on the balance, because an un-wagered match artificially holds every payout and would make a fair operator look slow. We also log any support ticket we open, so a single anomalous run does not skew the ranking — one operator that took six hours once is noted in the 90th percentile, not treated as its median. This is the timestamped evidence no competitor publishes.

How to Speed Up Your Own Withdrawal

You control more of your payout speed than you think. Pre-verify your ID at registration, not at cashout — uploading your driver's licence and a utility bill the day you sign up removes the single biggest delay. Second, use PayID and make sure the name on your casino account exactly matches the name on your PayID; a mismatch triggers a manual review ticket every time. Third, clear any bonus wagering before you withdraw, otherwise the operator holds the payout while the bonus is still active.

Fourth, avoid weekend and public-holiday cuts: request payouts on a weekday morning so they land inside a working review window. Fifth, keep your withdrawal amount within the operator's standard limit — very large sums often move to a senior reviewer even at fast operators. Finally, screenshot the confirmation and keep the transaction reference so a "where is my money" ticket takes five minutes to resolve instead of an hour. The best payment methods guide covers which rail to choose.

On the documents themselves, a clear licence photo and a recent utility bill or bank statement that matches your name and address are what clear verification fastest. Blurry ID, an expired document, or a statement older than three months is the usual reason a fast operator still asks for a re-submit. If you deposited by card at some point, expect the operator to ask for that card too before a large payout — another reason PayID is the cleaner path from the start. Do all of this before you have a win in your pocket, not when you are anxiously waiting for it.

Why is my fast withdrawal still pending?

Pending almost always means internal operator review, not a problem with your bank. The common causes are unfinished KYC (ID not uploaded or rejected), an active bonus not yet fully wagered, or a name mismatch between your casino account and your PayID (the two must match exactly). Less often, a large withdrawal triggers a manual risk review. Fix these before requesting: upload your ID early, clear or forfeit the bonus, and confirm your legal name matches. Once cleared, PayID moves in 60 seconds to 15 minutes for established accounts. See our PayID guide.

Do withdrawal times include weekends?

The PayID network itself moves money 24/7 — it never sleeps — but operator review teams are smaller on weekends and public holidays. A withdrawal requested Friday night often sits until Monday morning before a human even looks at it, even though the bank side would clear in minutes. The fastest play is to request your payout on a weekday morning between 9am and 3pm. Established, pre-verified accounts sometimes get auto-approved around the clock, but do not count on it. If you need cash before the weekend, request it by Thursday afternoon. First withdrawals still take 24–72 hours regardless.

Is instant withdrawal without any verification safe?

No. Every reputable operator asks for ID eventually, and a site that promises unlimited instant cashouts with zero checks is usually hiding something — often rigged games or a plan to withhold your first big payout. The safe pattern is to verify up front: upload your driver licence or passport and a bank statement the day you join, so your first withdrawal is fast rather than delayed. Verify now and you get the speed; skip it and you get a three-day hold. Pair this with our licence check and you avoid the rogue operators entirely. 18+ only.

Does the rejected-payout rate matter?

Yes, enormously. A fast median payout time with a high rejection rate means roughly one in five of your cashouts gets stuck in review, sent back, or asked for more documents — which turns a 15-minute withdrawal into a three-day ordeal. That is why BPAAU reports the rejection rate alongside median speed, not just the headline number. Pick an operator whose speed is reliable week after week, not one that is fast on a lucky day. Pre-verifying your ID and using PayID in your own name cuts rejections sharply regardless of operator. 18+ only.
