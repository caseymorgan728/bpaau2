The Game

What Is Gate of Olympus?

Gate of Olympus is a 6-reel, 5-row cluster-pays video pokie from Pragmatic Play, themed around Zeus and Mount Olympus. Unlike line-pay pokies, it pays when eight or more matching symbols land anywhere on the grid - the scatter-pay mechanic. Winning symbols explode and new ones tumble in, so a single spin can chain multiple wins. The headline mechanic is the multiplier symbols: golden orb symbols that land with random values up to 500x, and during the free-spin round those multipliers accumulate. The 1XACE high-RTP list shows this title at 97.06% RTP, which is at the top of what a mainstream pokie pays.

Volatility is high - the tumble mechanic means long base-game spells punctuated by big chained wins. The 5,000x max win means at a $100 bet that is a $500,000 single-spin ceiling. Pragmatic Play also offers a buy free spins option (typically 100x the bet), which lets you skip the base game and go straight to the multiplier-accumulating round.
