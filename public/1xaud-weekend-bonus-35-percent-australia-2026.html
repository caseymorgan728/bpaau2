The Fine Print

Terms, Rollover & Key Conditions

Like the daily bonus, the weekend match uses rollover: the total amount you must wager equals your deposit plus the bonus, multiplied by the published requirement. For example, a $100 deposit with a $35 bonus at a 10x rollover means roughly $1,350 in total bets before withdrawal. The exact multiplier is shown on the promotions tab when the weekend window opens, so check it before you load.

The rules that matter most are no stacking, time window and pokies-only contribution. You cannot combine the 35% weekend match with the daily 25%, a free credit, the Pokies Unlimited rebate or a referral commission at the same time. Deposits made outside the weekend window do not qualify, and live dealer or table games contribute 0% toward rollover. KYC is completed once before first withdrawal; 18+ only.
