Research & Data

The BPAAU editorial team produces ongoing research into online pokies, bonus economics and the Australian igaming market. Our findings inform our reviews and help players make better decisions.

Our Research Methodology

BPAAU research combines hands-on casino testing with data analysis. We collect withdrawal times, wagering requirement outcomes, RTP verification data and player feedback across multiple operators. Our team tests in real AUD to produce findings that reflect what Australian players actually experience.

We do not use sponsored data. All research is conducted independently. Where we cite external studies or statistics, we attribute the source.

Our Research Process

Our research process begins with real-money testing at each operator we cover. A team member registers a new account, claims the advertised bonus, and plays through the stated wagering requirement on a mix of eligible pokies. We log deposit and withdrawal timestamps, game outcomes, and any discrepancies between the terms page and the actual cashier flow. This hands-on testing is repeated quarterly so that seasonal bonus changes, KYC slowdowns and new payment methods are caught before they end up in a stale guide.

Beyond direct testing, we audit bonus terms line by line. We record the wagering multiplier, game weighting table, maximum bet while wagering, expiry window and maximum cashout for every offer, then calculate the expected player value under realistic play conditions. For payout speeds, we compare the operator's stated processing window against our own bank transaction logs, noting any delays caused by first-verification checks or weekend cut-offs. Where an operator's advertised RTP conflicts with independent audit data, we flag the discrepancy rather than repeat the marketing figure.

Data Sources & Methodology

Our primary sources are the operators themselves. We register, deposit and withdraw in real AUD, and we keep dated screenshots of bonus terms, cashier screens and completed withdrawal confirmations. We also consult public regulatory registries, including the Gaming Curacao licence database, to confirm that an operator holds a current licence before we list it. For payment timing, we rely on our own bank transaction logs showing when funds actually arrive in the player account, rather than the operator's stated turnaround.

Secondary sources include published industry reports, regulatory filings from bodies such as the Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA), and independent pokie audit reports from testing houses such as eCOGRA and iTech Labs. Where we cite external statistics, we attribute the source directly. Every figure that enters our guides is cross-checked against at least two sources: our own testing and a public record. If those sources disagree, we note the discrepancy and recommend that players verify the current terms on the operator's own site before claiming anything.

How We Use Research to Help Players

Research on BPAAU is not an end in itself. Every benchmark, term breakdown and payout log feeds directly into our guide content. When our quarterly testing shows that PayID withdrawals at one operator have slowed from 20 minutes to over two hours, we update that operator's review and our fast payout comparison within days. When a bonus's wagering multiplier changes, we recalculate the expected value and revise the relevant bonus guide.

The same research also powers our educational articles. Our high-RTP pokie recommendations are built on the RTP verification project; our responsible-gambling coverage is informed by how self-exclusion tools are actually implemented offshore. You can read the results in our blog, and learn more about who runs this work on our About page. If you are a player and want to understand why a particular operator is ranked the way it is, our research records explain the reasoning behind every conclusion.
