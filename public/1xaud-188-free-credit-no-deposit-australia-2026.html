The Fine Print

Terms, Wagering & Key Conditions

All promotions on this white-label network sit under shared terms. Withdrawals require meeting a minimum turnover or win-multiplier per promotion. On this platform, turnover is calculated as your ending balance multiplied by the requirement — for example, a $50 credit at a 3x requirement means you can withdraw once the balance reaches roughly $150. This is different from rollover, which counts total wagers placed (deposit plus bonus times the multiplier). Free credits and rebates are valid exclusively on pokies and slots; table games, live dealer and crash titles do not count toward turnover.

Two rules will bite you if you miss them. First, no stacking: you cannot combine this free credit with a deposit bonus, rebate or commission at the same time. Second, bonus abuse — saving free spins, exploiting a bug, or running multiple accounts — forfeits all remaining credits. KYC (identity and proof of address) is completed once, usually before the first withdrawal, and all players must be 18 or over.
