Overview

What Is Sweet Bonanza?

Sweet Bonanza is a brightly coloured pokie from Pragmatic Play set on a 6-reel, 5-row grid of fruits and candies. It abandoned traditional paylines in favour of a scatter-pays / pays-anywhere engine: land eight or more matching symbols anywhere and they pay, regardless of position. The winning symbols then vanish and new ones tumble into the gaps, so a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins.

The combination of chain-reaction tumbles and the free-spins multiplier bombs (up to 100x) gives it an extreme high-variance profile and a headline maximum win around 21,100x, which is why it features heavily in Australian free-spins promotions alongside Big Bass Bonanza.

The engine

Pays-Anywhere and Tumble Mechanics

  • No paylines. Eight or more of the same symbol anywhere on the grid trigger a win; twelve or more pay the top tier for that symbol.
  • Symbol tiers. Fruits (banana, grapes, watermelon, plum, apple) are the low payers; coloured candies pay more, with the red heart the highest.
  • Tumbles. Winning symbols explode and symbols above fall into place; new tumbles continue until no winning combination remains, all within the one original bet.
  • Lollipop scatter. Four or more trigger free spins in any position.
The feature

Free Spins and the 100x Multiplier Bombs

  1. Trigger — 4+ lollipop scatters award 10 free spins.
  2. Multiplier bombs — during the round, candy-bomb symbols with values from 2x to 100x can land.
  3. Applied after tumbles — when a spin’s tumble chain ends, the values of all bombs present are added together and multiply the spin’s total win.
  4. Retrigger — 3 more scatters during the round add 5 extra free spins, with no hard cap.

Large results require a long tumble chain capped by a high multiplier bomb (or several combined) — rare, which is exactly why the game is high volatility.

RTP warning

Sweet Bonanza is one of the titles where operators may be offered more than one return-to-player configuration. The Ante Bet increases the stake (typically +25%) to roughly double the natural chance of triggering free spins by adding more scatter symbols to the reels — a trade of higher cost for faster feature access.
