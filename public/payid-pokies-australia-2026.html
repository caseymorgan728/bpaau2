The Basics

What Is PayID and How Does It Work for Pokies?

PayID is an alias set up through your Australian bank that points to your real BSB and account number. Instead of handing a pokie site a raw account number, you give them your PayID — usually your mobile number or email — and the bank's New Payments Platform (NPP) routes the money to the right account. The underlying rail is Osko, which is why withdrawals sometimes show as "OSKO" on a pokie site. The two names refer to the same fast-payments system.

For pokie players the practical difference is large. A classic bank transfer can take one to three business days, because it queues in the batch clearing cycle. PayID/Osko settles in seconds because the NPP is a 24/7 real-time rail. Deposits reflect in your pokie balance as soon as the operator's gateway sees the incoming payment; withdrawals clear once the operator approves them and releases them to the NPP.

Why are pokie sites excited about it? Because it removes the two biggest friction points for Australian players: waiting days for a withdrawal, and exposing a card number to an offshore operator. With PayID you share an alias, not card details, and the money moves on the bank's own secure network. That is also why PayID is the default withdrawal rail on every operator we track.

Step by Step

How to Deposit and Withdraw at a PayID Pokie Site

The PayID flow is almost identical across the six operators, because they share the same white-label banking gateway. The variable is your own preparation: name matching and KYC.

  1. Set up a PayID in your own banking app (Mobile > PayID > register your phone number or email to the account you will use for pokies).
  2. Register at your chosen pokie site using the same legal name that appears on your ID, bank statement and PayID. This name must match across all three.
  3. Go to the deposit page, choose bank transfer / PayID, and copy the current operator bank account and Reference ID. Bank accounts rotate, so always copy the latest details.
  4. Send the deposit from your own banking app using that PayID or account + reference. Use your full name on the transfer and avoid sensitive words like "casino" or "pokies" on bank slips.
  5. For withdrawals, request the payout to your PayID-linked account. After approval the NPP moves the money in seconds; the operator approval is the waiting part.

Fastest Cashouts

How to Get the Fastest Possible PayID Payout in 2026

Four habits make a real difference to payout speed, and none of them require special status. First, complete KYC at registration — not at cashout. Upload your photo ID and a recent utility bill or bank statement the day you sign up; that single step removes the most common delay. Second, match the names: pokie account name, bank account name and PayID name must all be identical. A mismatch triggers a manual review.

Third, withdraw under the review threshold for your first payout. Smaller withdrawals tend to clear without manual review, which is why no-deposit winnings often arrive faster than a big real-money win. Fourth, avoid peak windows: payouts requested late Friday or over the weekend can sit pending until business hours. Request payouts on weekday mornings when the operator's finance team is active. Hold the transaction reference for every payout — it is what support uses to trace a missing payment. Our same-day withdrawals guide and under-1-hour withdrawals page go deeper.
