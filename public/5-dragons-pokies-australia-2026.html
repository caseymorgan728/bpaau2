Features

Bonus Features, Free Spins & Special Symbols

5 Dragons' free-spin multiplier choice is the reason it has endured for over a decade:
  • Dragon Scatters: Three or more dragon symbols trigger the free-spin choice round.
  • Five Multiplier Options: Pick from a range of free-spin counts paired with win multipliers - the classic 3x, 5x, 8x, 10x and 15x choices Aussies know.
  • Re-Triggers: Landing more scatters inside the free-spin round re-triggers additional spins.
  • Hold & Spin: Some Megah5 adaptations add a hold-and-spin respin layer on top of the classic free spins.
There is no progressive jackpot; the big wins are the multiplier applied to free-spin line wins. The choice of multiplier is the strategic depth - a high multiplier pays more per win but hits less often.
Strategy

Which free-spin multiplier should I pick in 5 Dragons?

Pick the higher-multiplier options (10x or 15x) if you want fewer but bigger free-spin wins, and the lower-multiplier options (3x or 5x) if you want more frequent smaller hits to keep the balance alive. The 8x option is the middle ground. For bonus wagering, the lower multiplier keeps spins going longer; for a single big hit, the 15x is the high-variance choice.
