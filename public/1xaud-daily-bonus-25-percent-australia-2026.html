The Fine Print

Terms, Rollover & Key Conditions

Deposit bonuses on this platform use rollover, which is different from the ending-balance turnover applied to no-deposit free credits. Rollover counts the total amount you wager: deposit plus bonus, multiplied by the requirement. For example, if you deposit $100, receive $25, and the rollover is 10x, you need to place roughly $1,250 in total bets before withdrawing. Always check the published multiplier on the promotions tab, as it can vary by offer.

The two non-negotiable rules are no stacking and pokies-only contribution. You cannot hold the daily bonus, the weekend bonus, a free credit and a rebate at the same time — only one active bonus per deposit. Free credits and rebates are valid only on pokies and slots; live dealer and table games count 0% or a reduced rate, so grinding those wastes the bonus. Bonus abuse, multi-accounting or bug exploitation forfeits all credits. KYC is completed once before first withdrawal; 18+ only. Read our bonus terms guide to decode the clause properly.
