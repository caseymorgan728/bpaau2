Strategy

How does the bonus number work in Keno Bonus Number?

You pick numbers from a keno grid, then a draw drops. If a drawn bonus number is among your picks, your matching win is multiplied. The more numbers you pick, the higher the top payout - but the harder it is to match them all. This bonus-number multiplier is why the game produces $2,400+ wins on the shared ticker.

Keno is a high-variance, math-driven game: picking more numbers raises the ceiling but sharply reduces the probability of a full match. For a bankroll-first strategy, pick a medium number of selections (4-6) where the paytable still pays for partial matches. Do not chase a full ten-number match - the expected value is worse than a medium pick.
