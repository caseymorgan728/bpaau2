How We Rank Top Pokies

Our top-pokies rankings are built on a consistent five-point framework so that one title can be compared fairly against another. First, we check the return-to-player (RTP) — the theoretical percentage of wagers returned over millions of spins — and confirm the figure against the pokie’s information screen rather than relying on marketing copy. Second, we assess volatility: how often and how large the wins are, because a high-RTP low-volatility game suits bonus wagering while a high-volatility title suits a single shot at a feature. Third, we evaluate bonus features — free spins, multipliers, cascades, bonus buy, hold-and-win respins — and how likely they are to actually trigger. Fourth, we consider the provider’s reputation for fair maths, licensed random number generators and a track record of paying out jackpots. Fifth, we weigh player feedback from Australian forums and complaint channels to catch games that overheat, under-pay or behave inconsistently.
