How to Read Each 2026 Bonus Number

Free Sign-Up Chips: $199, $177.77 and $100

The headline free chip is the amount credited to a new player's balance after registration, before any deposit. In 2026 the biggest sign-up credits on BPAAU are 1XAUD's $199 free and 1XACE's $177.77 free; GARCAT8 adds a $100 daily easy-step free credit structure. The number alone does not tell you the value — what matters is the wagering attached. A $199 chip with 40x play-through can be worth less than a $100 chip with a 10x requirement. Always open the no-deposit bonus guide and check the wagering column before claiming.

Daily and Weekly Free Credits: $18, $100 and Rebates

Daily free credits are the quiet workhorses of the 2026 Australian market. MR BLUEY credits $18 daily to active depositors and adds a 10% weekly win/loss rebate; GARCAT8 runs its daily easy-step free credit; GD8 pays a 1.18% rollover rebate across play. These recurring offers matter more than a one-time welcome bonus for players who deposit regularly — over a month, a reliable daily credit plus rebate usually outweighs a single bigger chip. Our free chips guide tracks which offers actually survive their terms.

Jackpot and VIP Bonuses: $2888, $1888.88 and $1888

Grand and premium jackpot bonuses are the high-water marks of each operator's reward ladder. GD8, MR BLUEY and TOY STORY 9 all feature a $2888 tier (grand jackpot or VIP upgrade); GARCAT8's signature jackpot bonus is $1888.88; 1XACE's premium jackpot is $1888; TOY STORY 9 also runs a lucky-wheel bonus up to $999. These are almost always tied to deposit milestones or VIP status, so treat them as loyalty rewards rather than new-player offers — and confirm the wagering before counting on the number.

Percentage Bonuses: 100%, 120% and No-Rollover Structures

Welcome and reload percentages multiply your deposit: 100% doubles it, 120% adds a 20% bonus on top. 1XAUD runs a 100% welcome and a 120% daily deposit bonus; 1XACE pairs its sign-up credit with a no-rollover 120% structure — meaning winnings from that bonus are not subject to the usual play-through, which is one of the strongest 2026 terms in the market. For the full comparison of which offers clear cleanly, see the 1XAUD review and 1XACE review.
