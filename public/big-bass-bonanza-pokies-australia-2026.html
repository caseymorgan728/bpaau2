Overview

What Is Big Bass Bonanza?

Big Bass Bonanza is an online pokie from Pragmatic Play with a cheerful fishing theme. It uses a classic 5 reels, 3 rows and 10 fixed paylines, with a relaxed cartoon lake setting and a soundtrack of splashes and reels. Its appeal is concentrated almost entirely in the free-spins feature, where the fisherman wild “catches” money-bearing fish and a retriggable multiplier ladder can build large single wins.

Its combination of simple base game, understandable feature and a high-volatility payout profile made it a default choice for Australian free-spins promotions, alongside titles such as Sweet Bonanza and Gates of Olympus.

How to play

Paylines, Bets and Symbols

Wins pay left-to-right on 10 fixed lines when three or more matching symbols land from the first reel. The low tier is the standard card ranks; the high tier is fishing tackle — a dragonfly, a fishing rod, a float, a tackle box and a hooked bass. Two special symbols drive the bonus:

  • Green bass (money fish). During free spins, fish carry random cash values that are only paid when a fisherman wild appears.
  • Golden fishing rod / scatter. Three or more trigger the free-spins round anywhere on the reels.

Stakes are adjustable to suit small bankrolls, and the game includes an optional autoplay with loss limits. Use these controls rather than chasing the feature at maximum stake.

The feature

Free Spins, the Fisherman Wild and the 10x Ladder

This is the heart of the game:

  1. Trigger — 3, 4 or 5 scatters award 10, 15 or 20 free spins.
  2. Collect — each fisherman wild that lands collects the cash values of every fish symbol currently on screen.
  3. Progress bar — fishermen are collected above the reels. Every 4th fisherman retriggers the round with 10 extra spins.
  4. Multiplier ladder — the first retrigger applies a 2x multiplier to collected fish, the second 3x, the third 10x.

The biggest results come from reaching the 10x level with high-value fish and a fisherman on the same spin. Because that requires stacking multiple retriggers, the feature is deliberately hard to complete — the definition of high volatility.

Big Bass Bonanza is a fishing-themed online pokie from Pragmatic Play, released in 2020, built on a 5-reel, 3-row, 10-fixed-payline layout with high volatility.
