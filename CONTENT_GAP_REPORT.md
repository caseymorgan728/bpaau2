## 8. 2026 Advanced SEO opportunities

### 8.1 Core Web Vitals (Google Search Console thresholds)
- **INP < 200 ms, LCP < 2.5 s, CLS < 0.1.**
- Practical moves for bpaau.org:
  - Lazy-load every operator logo, popout banner and jackpot image; use `fetchpriority="high"` only on the above-the-fold hero.
  - Preload the six operator logos that appear in the first viewport.
  - Defer non-critical JS (analytics, chat popups).
  - Reserve `width`/`height` on every image and banner to kill CLS.
  - Serve modern formats (WebP/AVIF — already used for `logo 2.webp` and `logo 5.webp`); convert PNG logos to WebP where possible.
  - Fonts: self-host or preconnect; avoid FOIT with `font-display: swap`.
  - The Cloudflare Worker (`_worker.js`) can edge-cache HTML and images — enable cache TTL ≥ 1 hour on article pages, purge on edit.

### 8.2 Mobile-first indexing
- bpaau.org must design every new page mobile-first. The majority of AU pokie traffic is already mobile.
- Recommended viewport behaviour:
  - Hero: one H1 + one CTA button ("Compare the six operators") above the fold.
  - Comparison tables: horizontally scrollable on mobile, not squeezed to 4pt text.
  - Product cards: 1-column on < 768px, 2-column on ≥ 768px, 3-column on ≥ 1024px.
  - Tap targets ≥ 44×44 px (Apple HIG) / 48×48 px (Android).
  - Sticky bottom CTA on mobile: "Compare top AU pokies bonuses" — not intrusive, dismissible.

### 8.3 Content freshness & "last updated" dating strategy
- Every article must show `Last updated: <Month Year>` in the byline block, **not** `Published on`.
- Bonus / payout / jackpot articles: **re-check monthly** (offers change fast). Re-date + touch the numbers even if text is unchanged.
- Evergreen articles (legality, RTP explainer, beginner guide): **re-check quarterly**.
- Progressive jackpot page: **embed live numbers**, then update the "last refreshed" timestamp automatically via the Worker.
- Use `dateModified` in `Article` schema for every update.
- Add a short "What changed in 2026?" callout box at the top of evergreen pages so Google (and users) see the freshness signal immediately.

### 8.4 Semantic HTML & accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)
- One `<h1>` per page; H2/H3 hierarchy without skips.
- Use `<nav aria-label="Breadcrumb">` and `<figure>`/`<figcaption>` for tables and product cards.
- All images carry descriptive `alt` (e.g. "1XAUD logo — Sign Up Free $199, Daily Deposit Bonus 120%").
- Colour contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on body text; ≥ 3:1 on large text.
- Skip-to-content link at top.
- Keyboard-accessible dropdowns, modal product popups, and comparison table filters.
- Use `<details>`/`<summary>` for FAQ blocks (better than custom JS accordions for SEO and a11y).

### 8.5 Keyword clustering & topical authority
- Group every keyword in `keyword_research.json` into one of the 13 topic clusters (already done in the JSON).
- Each cluster has **one pillar page** that links out to every child; every child links back to the pillar with exact-match anchor.
